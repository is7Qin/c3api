# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Thank you for contributing to c3api. This page explains the contribution terms and how to sign.

## Why a CLA

c3api is **dual-licensed**: the open-source [AGPL-3.0-or-later](./LICENSE) license and a paid [commercial license](./LICENSE.commercial). To allow external code contributions to be merged under this dual-license scheme, contributors sign this CLA.

The contribution terms are aligned with the English [LICENSE.commercial](./LICENSE.commercial), Section 4 (Miscellaneous):

> **Contributor terms**: accepting external code contributions requires signing a CLA (a contributor copyright assignment agreement), which warrants that the copyright of the contributed code is vested in the Licensor and that it may be incorporated into this dual-license scheme. Please contact us via an issue in the repository before contributing.

## What signing means

By signing this CLA, you agree to the contributor terms quoted above verbatim — in particular:

- the copyright of your contributed code is vested in the Licensor (is7Qin), and the contribution may be incorporated into the dual-license scheme of c3api (AGPL-3.0-or-later and commercial);
- contributions include code, documentation, and other material you submit to the repository (pull requests, issues, and comments to the extent they are incorporated).

## Voluntary and one-time

- **Voluntary** — signing is entirely voluntary; please only sign if you agree with the terms. You may decline, in which case your contribution cannot be merged.
- **One-time, lifetime** — you sign **once**, and the CLA applies to all your current and future contributions to c3api. There is no per-PR signing.

## How to sign (automated)

Signing is handled automatically by the CLA Assistant workflow (`.github/workflows/cla.yml`):

1. Open a pull request to c3api.
2. The bot posts a comment on the PR with instructions (if you have not signed yet).
3. Reply in a comment with the exact sign phrase: `I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA`.
4. The signature is recorded in the `cla-signatures` branch; the CLA status check turns green for this and all future PRs.
